Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Contact: heyiamshiva@gmail.com

This policy explains how ZarqGames handles information in Thirsty Tongue, our Android puzzle game, and how third-party services used by the game may handle information. It describes the current closed-testing version.

At a glance: Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. No game account is required. The current Android test build requests an age before initializing ads. Players who indicate they are under 18, or choose not to provide an age, do not receive ads in this build. Adults may receive Google demonstration ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores your current level, completed puzzles, discoveries, Drops balance, hint and reward state, and sound/music preferences locally so you can resume playing. These records are not uploaded to a ZarqGames gameplay server, and we do not provide cloud saving or a public online leaderboard in this version.

The age number you enter is used on your device to determine an age group. The game saves only adult, under 18, or unknown; it does not retain your exact age or ask for your date of birth. This age group is not sent to a ZarqGames server.

2. Advertising and third-party SDK data

The closed-testing build contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK, integrated through the Poing Studios Godot AdMob plugin. It uses Google's demonstration ad units rather than live monetized ad units. Demonstration ads can still involve connections to Google services; test mode should not be interpreted as an absence of data processing.

When the ad SDK operates for eligible adult players, Google may process an IP address (which can indicate a general location), ad/app interaction information, diagnostic and performance information, and device or app identifiers, depending on the SDK version and settings. Such processing supports ad delivery, measurement, service operation, security and fraud prevention. Google describes its SDK data practices in its Mobile Ads data-disclosure documentation and Privacy Policy.

Our adult test-ad requests are configured as non-personalized and limited to a maximum G ad-content rating. The Android build configuration removes the advertising-ID permission and related AdServices identifier, Topics and custom-audience permissions. These restrictions do not mean that all other SDK information, such as IP addresses or app-set identifiers, is absent.

ZarqGames has not integrated Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics for gameplay-event tracking. Advertising SDK measurement is separate from such an integration.

3. Children and players of unknown age

Thirsty Tongue is intended for both children and adults. We use a neutral age entry screen without a preselected age. In this closed-testing version, a player who enters an age below 18, or chooses “Prefer not to say,” can play without advertising. The game does not explicitly initialize the advertising SDK or request ads for these players, and it hides rewarded-ad offers from them.

The under-18 cutoff is our conservative test-build setting; it is not a claim that the legal definition of a child is the same everywhere. We do not ask children to provide a name, email address, account or other contact details to play. A parent or guardian can contact us with privacy questions.

4. Information you send to us

If you email ZarqGames for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal details. A parent or guardian should contact us on a child's behalf.

5. Sharing and permissions

We do not sell personal information. The game does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Network access is used for the advertising features described above. Google independently handles information processed by its advertising services under its applicable terms and privacy notices. We may disclose support correspondence if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, security or legal rights.

If this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, visiting the policy website also involves GitHub's web-hosting services, which may process ordinary connection information such as an IP address. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. This policy page itself contains no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or contact form.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain on your device until you clear the game's app storage or uninstall it, subject to your device's storage behavior. Clearing app storage also removes saved progress, Drops, settings and the saved age group. The age screen will appear again when you start with cleared storage.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to respond, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion of correspondence we hold, subject to those obligations. We cannot directly delete information Google controls; Google's privacy tools and policies explain the choices it provides. There is no game account to delete in this version.

7. Security and international processing

We limit the information the game stores and use the platform's app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Google states that data its Mobile Ads SDK transmits is encrypted in transit. Google and email or hosting providers may process information in countries outside your own, under their respective safeguards and policies.

8. Your choices and privacy questions

You may choose not to provide an age and continue playing. You can remove local information through Android's app-storage settings. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restrictions relating to personal information we hold. Contact us to make a request. Please identify the relevant request without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our data practices change. Changes such as live advertising, additional analytics, online accounts or new data-sharing features require a fresh review and an updated policy and store disclosure where applicable.
